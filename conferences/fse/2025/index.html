FSE 2025 Acceptance Rate

FSE 2025 accepted 135 papers out of 612 submissions, for an acceptance rate of 22.1%.

ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering 2025 (33rd) was held in Tronheim, Norway.

Compared with FSE 2024, the acceptance rate decreased from 25.5% to 22.1%, and submissions increased from 474 to 612.
