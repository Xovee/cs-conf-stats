ASE 2025 Acceptance Rate

ASE 2025 accepted 245 papers out of 1,136 submissions, for an acceptance rate of 21.6%.

ACM/IEEE International Conference on Automated Software Engineering 2025 (40th) was held in Seoul, Korea.

Compared with ASE 2024, the acceptance rate decreased from 27.3% to 21.6%, and submissions increased from 568 to 1,136.
