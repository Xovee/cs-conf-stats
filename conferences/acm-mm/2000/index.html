ACM MM 2000 Acceptance Rate

ACM MM 2000 accepted 36 papers out of 214 submissions, for an acceptance rate of 16.8%.

ACM International Conference on Multimedia 2000 (8th) was held in Marina de Rey, California, USA.

Compared with ACM MM 1997, the acceptance rate decreased from 28.2% to 16.8%, and submissions increased from 142 to 214.
