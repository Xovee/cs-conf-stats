ICRA 2026 Acceptance Rate

ICRA 2026 accepted 1,882 papers out of 4,947 submissions, for an acceptance rate of 38.0%.

IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation 2026 (43rd) was held in Vienna, Austria.

Compared with ICRA 2025, the acceptance rate decreased from 38.7% to 38.0%, and submissions increased from 4,153 to 4,947.
