ISSTA 2025 Acceptance Rate

ISSTA 2025 accepted 107 papers out of 550 submissions, for an acceptance rate of 19.5%.

ACM SIGSOFT International Symposium on Software Testing and Analysis 2025 (34th) was held in Tronheim, Norway.

Compared with ISSTA 2024, the acceptance rate decreased from 20.6% to 19.5%, and submissions decreased from 694 to 550.
